> Learning in public. Weekly entries documenting my PM transition — what I learned, why it matters, how I'm applying it.

---

## Why This Exists

Writing accelerates learning. When you write about what you've learned, you are forced to find the gaps in your understanding. This repository is my public log of that process — honest, specific, and continuous.

This is not polished thought leadership. It is a working journal. The entries from week 1 will look less sophisticated than the entries from week 52. That arc is the point.
